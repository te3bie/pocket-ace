
# 🎓 Pocket Ace
### Student life, with a little more balance.

Pocket Ace helps Canadian students plan university costs, track everyday expenses, build savings goals, and explore educational opportunities—all in one friendly space.

## Try the demo

**[Open Pocket Ace →](https://te3bie.github.io/pocket-ace/)**

Available on your phone or computer. No installation needed.

## Getting started

1. **Take a look around**  
   Open the app and tap **Explore the fictional demo**.

2. **Make it yours**  
   On **Home**, tap **Edit Details** to enter your current balance.

3. **Plan ahead**  
   Open **Calendar** to set your budget and add upcoming payments.

4. **Find your balance**  
   Review your spending on **Home**, track savings goals in **Profile**, and explore example programs and scholarships in **Discover**.

Your demo changes are saved in the browser on your device.

## What’s inside

| Feature | How it helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Student budgeting** | Plan semester costs and weekly or monthly expenses. |
| **Payment calendar** | Track due dates, mark payments as paid, and see your remaining budget. |
| **Savings goals** | Follow your progress toward university costs or an emergency fund. |
| **Opportunity finder** | Explore fictional programs, scholarships, bursaries, and extracurricular opportunities. |
| **Application tracking** | Save opportunities and track your application status. |
| **PocketPal demo** | Try a scripted guide to student budgeting. |

## About this demo

Pocket Ace is a working demo with fictional data.

Live AI, bank connections, and verified opportunity listings are not enabled. PocketPal uses scripted responses, and opportunity listings are examples for demonstration purposes.
